So I started study Android Application for 1 year.

In third grade, there's nothing I write because activites in third grade are very static. Step 3: Multimodal Work Explanation

⚠️ Minimum 200 words required. This section reflects my independent critical thinking.

I chose to make a website instead of a PPT or video because I studied web development in high school. This project was a great chance to use my HTML and CSS skills. Building a website from scratch allowed me to control the design and integrate my story into one link cleanly. To add meaning to my story, I used three multimedia parts. For Grade 2, the multimedia shows my project exhibitions at COEX and EXCO, and the dormitory life. Most importantly, I used my computer programming skills as a multimedia option to build an interactive timeline. I coded a dynamic navigation bar and a scroll system to show my three years of high school visually. This timeline helps readers see how my identity changed from a young student to a professional developer. For literary techniques, I used a big "tone shift." The story starts funny, like cooking boiled meat in the dorm, but changes to dark and serious when I talk about quitting my first job. Also, I wrote this text just like I am talking to a friend. I used words like "But," "And," or "Why?" at the start of sentences because I wanted to show my real feelings, not a boring academic essay. I think these simple and honest words make my story more real.
